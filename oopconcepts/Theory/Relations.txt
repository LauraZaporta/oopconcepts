TYPES OF RELATIONS (Betwween objects):
--------------------------------------

- Association: Defines a simple connection between two objects. For example, imagine We have the object Pigeon and the object Tree and the relation stablished between the
  Two is Pigeon LIVES IN Tree.

- Composition: Relation defined as a part-of-everything where each part of the relation can't exist or doesn't make sense to exist outside the everything. For example,
  the trunk of a tree is paart of the tree but doesn't make much sense existing outside of It.

- Agregation: Relation defined as a part-of-everything similaar to composition but "weaker" (each part of everything can exist without the everything). For example, an apple
  of an apple tree is part of the tree. However, It can exist outside of It.

- Inheritance: Explained previously in the .txt "InheritanceAndPolymorphism".
